# Bug report

Bugs in the clinic's AI receptionist, found by listening to the recordings in `calls/`. Every entry points to a call folder and a timestamp; the timestamp is the same in that call's `transcript.txt` and `recording.ogg`.

`bug_candidates.md` (from `py -m caller review`) was a starting point. Nothing goes in here unless I heard it in the recording.

## Entry format

Replace the example below with real entries. Four or five clear bugs are better than a long list of small ones.

## 1. Short title of the bug

Call: `calls/<folder>/transcript.txt` at 1:23

What happened: what the receptionist said or did, quoted where possible.

What should have happened: the behavior a patient would expect.

Why it matters: the effect on a real patient (wrong booking, privacy, bad advice, wasted time).

Severity: high / medium / low
